## 🌏 Who Are We?

MeshCN is currently the largest Meshtastic community with the most Chinese-speaking users (中文用户最多的 Meshtastic 社区). While the group is international and welcomes English, French, Turkish, and other speakers (WeChat has auto-translation), it is mainly oriented toward people in and around China—including Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and overseas Chinese communities.

- 500 members in our first WeChat group (maxed out)

- About 200 members in a second WeChat group

- 150 members in a QQ group

That’s a total of over 670 active users, sharing ideas, helping each other configure nodes, and expanding Meshtastic mesh networks across regions.

## 🇭🇰 Active Members from Hong Kong

We currently have around 5 active members from Hong Kong (香港), and they’ve been *very* helpful to the community. Some of them are from Yuen Long, Tin Shui Wai, and Hong Kong Island, covering both the New Territories and urban areas.

Some of them have:

- Set up multiple Meshtastic nodes in Hong Kong,

- Shared their experiences about building and optimizing long-range links in urban environments,

- Helped new users (both local and abroad) with setup and frequency selection.

🛠 Important Note for Hong Kong Users:  

Hong Kong does not use the Mainland China 470–510 MHz ISM band (中国大陆的 470–510 MHz ISM 频段).  

Instead, Hong Kong users typically configure their Meshtastic region as `TW` (Taiwan), since they share the same 920-925 MHz ISM band.

If you’re in Hong Kong, remember to change the region setting to `TW` to ensure your Meshtastic device uses the correct frequency band.
